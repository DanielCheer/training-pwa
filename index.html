That's a ~ kcal surplus over your TEE (). Weigh in every Sun morning before the gym. Gaining faster than ~0.75 lb/week after week 3 = you're adding fat, trim 150–200 kcal. Stalling 2 weeks = add 150.
Meal Structure
Total calories and protein decide body composition — meal count alone doesn't burn more fat or build more muscle. But at ~ kcal / g protein a day, 4–5 meals beats 2–3: easier to actually eat the volume without forcing it, and spreading protein into ~50 g doses roughly every 3–4 hours gives your muscles more separate windows to use it instead of one or two big boluses.
